Service overview

An inspection that leaves you informed.

Too many inspections produce a folder of jargon. Ours produce a conversation: what is working, what is not, what it costs to fix and what can wait.

Every assessment is carried out by a senior engineer who will explain findings on site, photograph evidence and rank each item by real-world risk rather than paperwork convenience.

Typical: 1 site dayReport in 5 daysAll property types
What we examine

A systematic review of every layer.

Active protection

Detection coverage, alarm audibility, panel fault history, suppression readiness and extinguisher condition.

Passive protection

Fire doors, compartments, penetrations, structural protection and means of escape routes.

Management systems

Records, maintenance regimes, staff training, emergency plans and evidence of previous actions.

Our method

Four stages, nothing left to chance.

01

Pre-visit review

We study drawings, previous reports and your logs before arriving, so site time is spent inspecting — not reading.

02

Walkthrough

A room-by-room survey with sample tests, photographs and immediate verbal feedback on critical issues.

03

Analysis

Findings mapped against current codes and ranked by likelihood and consequence, with practical remedies.

04

Report & review

A clear written report and a call to walk through it — including an honest view of what does not need doing.
