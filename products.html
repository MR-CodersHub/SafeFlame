Buying guide

Four questions before you specify anything.

01

What is the hazard?

Fuel type, occupancy and processes determine extinguishing media more than price does.

02

Who is exposed?

Public, staff or vulnerable occupants change detection and evacuation requirements.

03

Can you test it?

Access for servicing is a design decision. Equipment nobody can reach will not stay compliant.

04

Who documents it?

Specify who issues certificates, maintains records and supports the system after handover.
