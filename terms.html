02 · Services

What our engagements cover.

Services are delivered by qualified engineers to the scope agreed in writing before work begins.

Design & installation

Detection, suppression, emergency lighting and signage installed to an agreed specification and commissioned with documented tests.

Inspection & reporting

Assessments and servicing performed on agreed dates, with findings and certificates issued in writing.

24/7 response

Emergency attendance for active incidents under the response terms included in your service agreement.

03 · Commercial

Quotations, pricing & payment.

We work to fixed, written prices so you know your exposure before any work starts.

Quotations are valid for thirty days from the date issued unless a different period is stated. Prices may change after that due to equipment or regulatory changes.

Work begins once you accept the written proposal, any required deposit is received and site access is confirmed. We will provide a scheduled start date.

We will not carry out extra chargeable work without your approval. Any new finding is documented and quoted separately before proceeding.

Monthly plans are rolling with thirty days' notice; annual plans are billed upfront. The scope and visit frequency are listed in your plan schedule.

04 · Responsibilities

What we need from you.

Site access & information

  • Provide safe access and relevant drawings or records when requested
  • Disclose changes to occupancy, hazards or systems that affect the work
  • Nominate a site contact for approvals and scheduling

After installation

  • Do not alter or disable installed equipment without consultation
  • Keep access to panels, valves and extinguishers unobstructed
  • Report faults, damage or alarm events promptly
