Emergency quick card

If a fire starts, remember four words.

01

Raise the alarm

Activate the nearest call point. Shout if you cannot reach one.

02

Get out

Use the nearest safe exit. Do not use lifts or collect belongings.

03

Close doors

Close doors behind you where safe — it slows fire and smoke spread.

04

Account for people

Go to the assembly point and report missing persons to the fire service.
