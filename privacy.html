Privacy Policy

How SafeFlame collects, uses and protects information when you request a quotation, use our client portal or visit this website.

Read the summary Privacy enquiry

At a glance

We collect only the details needed to provide fire protection services, maintain your compliance records and respond to enquiries. We do not sell personal data. You can request access, correction or deletion at any time.

Last updated: 27 November 2026
01 · Collection

Information we collect.

We keep collection limited to what is necessary for the service you ask us to provide.

Enquiry details

Name, company, email address, phone number and the information you provide about your site when you request a quotation, inspection or consultation.

Portal information

Account credentials, site records, service history, certificates and documents you or your team upload to the SafeFlame client portal.

Website usage

Basic, aggregated technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site secure and improve usability.

02 · Purpose

Why we use your information.

Every use is tied to delivering, documenting or improving our fire protection services.

To prepare quotations, schedule site visits and deliver inspection reports and certificates.
To maintain your asset register, service reminders and compliance records in the client portal.
To respond to emergency calls and coordinate engineer dispatch when you contact us.
To meet legal, insurance and audit obligations relating to fire safety documentation.
To send safety insights you have subscribed to, with an option to unsubscribe at any time.
03 · Sharing

Who we share it with.

We share information only where it is needed to deliver the service or where the law requires it.

RecipientPurposeWhat is shared
Assigned engineersSite delivery and servicingSite details and relevant records
Equipment manufacturersWarranty and technical supportProduct and installation identifiers
Insurers & authoritiesCompliance evidence, where requiredCertificates and inspection reports
Service providersHosting, email and portal operationOnly the data needed to perform their role

We never sell personal information. Third parties acting on our behalf are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.

04 · Retention & security

Kept only as long as needed.

Service and compliance records are retained for the period required by applicable fire safety and insurance obligations. Enquiry data that does not lead to a service engagement is kept only for a short follow-up period.

How we protect it

  • Role-based portal access with unique user accounts
  • Encrypted transmission of information submitted through the site
  • Controlled access to physical and digital records
  • Regular review of retention and access permissions
05 · Cookies

Simple, necessary cookies.

This site uses local storage to remember your light/dark theme and text direction preferences. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Preference storage

Remembers theme and RTL/LTR settings so your next visit looks the same.

Session security

Portal sessions use essential storage to keep your account signed in securely.

No ad tracking

We do not use advertising pixels, behavioural profiling or cross-site trackers.

06 · Your rights

You stay in control.

You may ask what we hold about you, request corrections, ask for deletion where retention is no longer required, or object to a particular use.

Make a request

Email response@safeflame.com with the subject “Data access request”. We will verify your identity and respond within one month.

Yes. Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe option, or you can ask us directly and we will remove your address.

Records required for fire safety or insurance obligations must be retained for their statutory period. We will explain any retention that applies to your request.

Questions

Need clarification on this policy?

Our team will answer plainly and tell you exactly what information we hold.
